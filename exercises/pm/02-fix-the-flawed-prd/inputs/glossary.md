# Glossary

Terms used in the Expense Fast Track PRD and its stories. Alphabetical. One line each.

## Approver

The person in the submitter's management chain who approves or rejects a claim, within their approval limit.

## Claim

A set of expense lines an employee submits together for reimbursement, in one currency.

## Delegate

An approver who takes over another approver's queue for set dates.

## Expense line

One expense on a claim: date, merchant, category, amount and an optional receipt.

## Receipt

A photo, image or PDF that proves an expense line.
