# EFT-04: Better approval screen

**Epic:** E2 Faster approvals

**Goal:** G2

**Size:** M

## Story

As an approver, I want a better approval screen so that approving is less painful.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC1: Screen is better

- **Given** an approver with claims waiting
- **When** they open the approval screen
- **Then** the experience feels faster and more intuitive

### AC2: Approvers are happy

- **Given** the new screen has shipped
- **When** we ask approvers in the next survey
- **Then** they are happier than before

## Edge Cases

- An approver with a lot of claims.
