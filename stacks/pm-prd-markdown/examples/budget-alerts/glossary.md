# Glossary

Terms used in the Budget Alerts PRD and its stories. Alphabetical. One line each.

## Active budgeter

A user who has at least one category limit and opened the app in the last 30 days.

## Alert

A push notification, plus a copy in the in-app Alerts inbox, about one category.

## Category limit

The monthly spending cap a budgeter sets for one spending category, in the account currency.

## Light tracker

A user with linked accounts who opens the app less than once a week.

## Muted category

A category whose alerts are switched off. Its limit and progress bar still work.

## Overspend alert

The alert sent when a category's spending goes above 100% of its limit.

## Threshold warning

The alert sent the first time in a month a category reaches 80% of its limit.
