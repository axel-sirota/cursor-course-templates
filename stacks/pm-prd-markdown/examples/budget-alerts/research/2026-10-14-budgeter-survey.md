# Research: Budgeter survey, October 2026

Fictional data for the course example.

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Method | In-app survey, 5 questions |
| Sample | 1,240 active budgeters |
| Dates | 2026-10-01 to 2026-10-10 |

## Findings

- 38% went over a category limit in September without noticing until month end.
- 61% open the Budget tab less than once a week.
- 22% of cancellations in Q3 named "I forgot to check" as the main reason.
- 71% said they would turn on alerts if they could pick which categories alert them.

## What this means for the PRD

- Supports G1 and the 38% baseline.
- The 71% finding is why BA-04 (mute per category) is in v1.
